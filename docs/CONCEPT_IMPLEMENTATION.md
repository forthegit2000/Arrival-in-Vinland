No feature in the right-hand column is presented as finished in the UI or documentation. In particular, land/work tenure currently changes consent, confidence and building ownership records; it is not a complete private-property market. The neighboring community is a transparent fictional aggregate, not a claim of cultural authenticity or a documented village at L'Anse aux Meadows.

## Research decisions

- Hall F keeps its six rooms and eastern lean-to. Room VII access is interpreted. The visual floor plan and roof shapes are schematic.
- The original 1005 arrival remains a scenario, while AD 1021 is identified as attested Norse activity.
- Barns/byres and experimental gardens are additions for this hypothetical settlement; the site has no identified livestock facilities.
- Construction figures remain nominal estimates with task breakdowns and an uncertainty range. Raw stock gathering is distinct from construction work. Crew skills, fatigue, climate and site access affect elapsed days.
- No neighboring society is automatically described as hostile, empty territory, or owned by discovery. No automatic historical warlord or year-triggered invasion is generated.
- Temperatures, yields, carrying capacities, ages and individual skills are game parameters, not false precision attributed to archaeological evidence.

## Validation

The automated model checks exercise the entire first seasonal year for a provisioned, housed trading harbor and compare fractional-clock updates with accelerated updates. Tests verify resource/crew conservation, meaningful assembly rejection, survey/voyage reservations, annual seasonal recovery, crop timing and save migration. The browser check opens `file://` with HTTP requests blocked and exercises the main game paths and multiple views. These checks provide concrete evidence for the implemented slice; they do not establish AAA production readiness or universal historical accuracy.
